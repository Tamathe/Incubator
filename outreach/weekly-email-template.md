## Weekly update checklist

- Confirm the date, time, time zone, and Teams link.
- Give the featured session one concrete description.
- Include no more than four future sessions.
- Show at least one open Friday when proposals are available.
- State who the session is for and whether preparation is required.
- Say whether the session will be recorded only when that is confirmed.
- Keep the Teams, sign-up, schedule, and proposal links as live text below the graphic.
- Add alt text to the graphic so the invitation still works when images are blocked.
